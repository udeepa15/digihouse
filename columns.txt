========================================================================
GOOGLE SHEETS COLUMNS FOR DIGIHOUSE APPLICATION FORMS
========================================================================

Below is the list of all columns required in your Google Sheet to capture
every field across both application types (Doctor and Office Workers),
including submission metadata.

Row 1 (Headers) should contain these exact columns in your sheet:

1.  Application Type       (e.g., doctor, office)
2.  Application Title      (e.g., Hospital Registration, Office Workers)
3.  Submitted At           (Timestamp of submission)
4.  Name with Initials      (nameWithInitials / fullName)
5.  NIC                    (nic)
6.  Gender                 (gender)
7.  Age                    (age)
8.  Date of Birth          (birthday)
9.  Designation / Position (designation / position)
10. Division / Ward        (divisionWard)
11. Employee Number        (employeeNo)
12. Department             (department)
13. Company Name           (companyName)
14. Years of Experience    (experienceYears)
15. Skills / Tools         (skills)
16. Address                (address)
17. Mobile / Phone Number  (mobileNumber / phone)
18. Email                  (email)
19. Signature Image URL    (signatureFile upload URL)
20. Photo Image URL        (photoFile upload URL)
21. Studio Photo No.       (studioPhotoNo)
22. Reference No.          (referenceNo)
